## Monsters (with game notes)


### Vampire
- Role: Elite stalker, drains life
- Abilities: Life drain bite (heals self), charm, shapeshift to bat or mist, summon thralls
- Weakness: Sunlight, running water, stakes through the heart, garlic
- Notes: Can't enter uninvited; Tier 2 variant has regen

### Werewolf
- Role: Brawler that transforms
- Abilities: Night form (fast, high melee), bleeding claws, howl that frightens nearby foes
- Weakness: Silver, full-moon vulnerability window
- Notes: Transforms on a timer or trigger; human form is weaker and hides it

### Goblin
- Role: Swarm fodder, trap setter
- Abilities: Pack tactics (bonus when flanking), crude explosives, steal items
- Weakness: Low HP, panics when leader dies
- Notes: Shaman variant buffs allies; boss variant is a goblin king

### Troll
- Role: Tank, regenerates
- Abilities: Heavy club smash, regeneration (stopped by fire or acid), ground stomp
- Weakness: Fire, acid, severed limbs stop regen
- Notes: Dies to sunlight in folklore; can be a game mechanic for a timed fight

### Wendigo
- Role: Hunger-driven horror
- Abilities: Hunger aura that drains allies' stamina, multi-hit claw swipes, cold breath
- Weakness: Fire, lure with food
- Notes: Gets stronger the more it consumes; good for a fear or sanity mechanic

### Kraken
- Role: Sea boss, grapple
- Abilities: Tentacle grab (pulls or holds), ink cloud (blinds), whirlpool
- Weakness: Must be hit on tentacles first; the head is exposed after the arms fall
- Notes: Multi-phase fight; best as a multi-arm boss

### Basilisk
- Role: Ranged or gaze threat
- Abilities: Petrifying gaze (must be reflected or blinded), venomous bite, serpent lash
- Weakness: Reflection (mirror), crowing rooster in lore, blind it
- Notes: Gaze could be a line-of-sight mechanic

### Mimic
- Role: Ambush trickster
- Abilities: Disguises as chest, door, or item; sticky tongue grab; hidden until touched
- Weakness: Fire on the disguise, reveal by probing
- Notes: Great for level design surprises

### Slime
- Role: Splitting ooze
- Abilities: Splits when hit, acid touch, absorbs items or weak enemies to grow
- Weakness: Cold (slows), large single hit (no splitting)
- Notes: Tiered: green, acid, king


### Ghoul
- Role: Corpse-eater, ambush pack hunter
- Abilities: Paralyzing claws, feast on the fallen to heal, tunneling
- Weakness: Fire, bright light, salt

### Lich
- Role: Undead sorcerer boss
- Abilities: Death magic, summon undead, phylactery (must be found and destroyed to kill it)
- Weakness: Phylactery, holy damage

### Cockatrice
- Role: Petrifying ambusher
- Abilities: Petrifying touch, venomous peck, flying short bursts
- Weakness: Weasel scent in lore, mirrors

### Chupacabra
- Role: Night predator of livestock
- Abilities: Drains blood, leaps, spiked back
- Weakness: Bright light, loud noise

### Mothman
- Role: Omen creature, herald of disaster
- Abilities: Flight, red-eye fear gaze, foretells events
- Weakness: Hard to hit while airborne; vulnerable when grounded

### Jersey Devil
- Role: Pine-barrens terror
- Abilities: Flight, shrieking, hooves and tail strikes
- Weakness: Iron, holy symbols
